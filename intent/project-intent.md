# Project Intent

Purpose:
Noise Signal is a personal iPhone app that tells you, at a glance, how loud the room is — a relative sound-level signal, not a calibrated meter.

Business outcomes:
- One tap to listen, one tap to stop; the answer (QUIET / NORMAL / LOUD / TOO LOUD) is readable from arm's length.
- Keeps a simple on-device history of finished sessions (Intent 002).

Constraints:
- SwiftUI, Swift concurrency, native Apple frameworks only. No dependencies.
- Microphone audio is measured, never recorded, stored, logged, or uploaded.
- No accounts, backend, analytics, ads, speech recognition, background listening, or health guidance.
- Built for personal use on a physical iPhone, not the App Store.
